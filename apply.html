Become a Life Progress Coach

Join the founding pilot cohort of student coaches. A small group of 4–6 students will be selected to receive training, practice supervised coaching, and help shape a new developmental peer coaching model at Global College.

What is Life Progress Coaching?

Life Progress Coaches walk alongside peers to listen deeply, ask thoughtful questions, and support meaningful forward movement under real-life constraints.

This is not psychotherapy or counseling. It is a developmental, non-clinical coaching approach focused on meaning, direction, agency, and supportive growth.

What You Will Gain

Coaching Skills

  • Learn how to listen deeply and ask better questions.
  • Practice the LPT framework and 4S coaching compass.
  • Develop ethical awareness and clear coaching boundaries.

Supervised Practice

  • Join an intensive training workshop.
  • Begin supervised coaching practice with real participants.
  • Receive feedback and supervision from the Head Coach.

Pilot Training & Practice Pathway

This pilot involves structured training, supervised coaching practice, and ongoing reflection over an extended period.

Participants are expected to make a consistent commitment across the full duration of the pilot.

Further details will be shared during the interview.

1

Apply & Interview

Complete the application form and participate in a 20-minute interview with the Head Coach. The conversation is designed to explore fit for the founding pilot cohort.

2

Train & Practice

Join the intensive training workshop, learn the LPC approach, and begin early supervised coaching practice with guidance and feedback.

3

Reflect & Contribute

Participate in supervision, submit session records, provide pilot feedback, and help improve the training and coaching system for future cohorts.

Who Should Apply?

We are looking for students who are:

  • Interested in coaching, education, leadership, communication, or personal growth.
  • Strong listeners who are open-minded and reflective.
  • Responsible, reliable, and willing to receive feedback.
  • Able to make a consistent commitment across the pilot period.
  • Open to learning developmental coaching skills in a non-clinical peer support model.

You do not need to:

  • Have all the answers.
  • Have prior coaching experience.
  • Act as a counselor or therapist.
  • Fix other people’s problems.

Founding Cohort Stipend

Participation Stipend

Up to 1,800 RMB

A stipend of up to 1,800 RMB will be provided to recognize the contribution of the founding cohort in the LPC pilot and validation phase.

The stipend is distributed in stages based on continued participation, supervised practice, and overall engagement in the training process.

This is not based solely on the number of sessions completed, but on the quality, continuity, and supervised development of coaching practice.

Details will be shared with selected participants during the interview and onboarding process.

Certification Path & Institutional Status

The Life Progress Center is currently in its Pilot & Development Phase. Students who successfully complete the training and supervised practice pathway may become pre-qualified for future LPC coach certification.

Formal certificate issuance is subject to the official establishment and administrative approval of the Center by the College.
